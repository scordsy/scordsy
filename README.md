### [skills](https://github.com/scordsy/skills): Claude skills for everyday sales and marketing jobs, checked before you rely on them

Three of them check work you already have. A target account list, an AI summary of a sales call or a draft with facts in it looks right, so it goes straight into the CRM, the outbound sequence or out the door. Often it is quietly wrong. When I asked ChatGPT for "50 companies like our best customer" and checked a sample (15), only 1 was usable.

The fourth, find-events, does the research for you: a structured search for the events worth attending to meet the people you want.

All four check their own work. Every finding comes with its source, and a separate checker tries to prove each finding wrong.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**check-account-list**](https://github.com/scordsy/skills/tree/main/skills/check-account-list) | Every company on a target account list checked before you spend on it: website, still trading, bought, size, fit and competitors, each with evidence you can click. |
| [**check-claims**](https://github.com/scordsy/skills/tree/main/skills/check-claims) | Every fact in your article, whitepaper or sales email traced to whoever first published it, before you publish or send it. |
| [**check-call-summary**](https://github.com/scordsy/skills/tree/main/skills/check-call-summary) | An AI summary of a call, checked claim by claim against the transcript before it goes in your CRM. |
| [**find-events**](https://github.com/scordsy/skills/tree/main/skills/find-events) | A shortlist of events worth attending to meet the people you want, with every event checked against the organiser's own page before you see it. |

Runs in Claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Claude Code. In Claude.ai or Claude Desktop, download a skill's zip and upload it: see [the install steps](https://github.com/scordsy/skills#claudeai-and-claude-desktop).

In Claude Code:

```bash
claude plugin install stefancordery-skills --marketplace scordsy/skills
```

### [list-decay](https://github.com/scordsy/list-decay): find the dead companies on your UK list

Find the dissolved and closing companies on your UK prospect list before you pay to enrich, verify or email them. Free, Python, nothing else to install. On a three-year-old list of 3,829 companies, 1 in 3 had gone or were going.
